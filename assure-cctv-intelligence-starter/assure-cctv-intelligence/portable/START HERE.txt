FRAMEWATCH 0.2 — RUN IT ON YOUR PC

1. If you are viewing the ZIP, close it, right-click Framewatch-PC.zip and
   choose Extract All. Open the extracted folder.
2. Double-click Framewatch.html. If necessary, right-click it and choose
   Open with > Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome. Use a current desktop browser.
3. Leave your CCTV viewer open. In Framewatch, click Share screen. Select the
   CCTV window (or the browser tab with the cameras), not Framewatch, and
   confirm Share in the browser prompt.
4. Choose the grid to match your screen: 2 x 2 means two columns and two rows.
   Use the crop tool if the camera viewer has menus or sidebars. Camera 01 is
   the upper-left view. Numbers run across each row.
5. Under Person detector, start with Balanced. If people look small in a
   distant view, try Detailed or enlarge the camera in the CCTV viewer. Use
   Fast on a slower PC. Last scan shows the actual time taken on your PC.
6. Read Camera status. Occupied means a person was detected. Empty means no
   person was detected, not proof that no one is there. Unclear means the
   view could not be classified reliably. On live video, Confirming vacancy
   means a second clear frame is needed. Click Stop sharing to finish.

NOTHING TO INSTALL
This is a local browser app. No command prompt, Node.js, account, access token,
web server, camera password, or internet connection is needed for analysis.
The sample shown at launch is an example, not your cameras. Screen capture
starts only when you choose a source. Close the tab to quit. To use it again,
open the same Framewatch.html file. You can create a desktop shortcut to it.

QUICK CHECK / TROUBLESHOOTING
- Click Open image and choose the included Example camera grid.webp. Set the
  grid to 2 x 2. It should show 01 and 03 occupied and 02 and 04 empty.
- If a browser or company policy does not allow live screen sharing, use
  Open image with a screenshot, or ask your IT administrator. Do not change
  security settings to bypass a restriction.
- If the local detector cannot run, try a current Edge or Chrome browser
  with WebGL available. Do not treat an unclear or frozen view as empty.
- This HTML file contains the person detector and its runtime. Frames stay
  in memory in the browser. No video/audio is uploaded or recorded.
