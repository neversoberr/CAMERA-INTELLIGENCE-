FRAMEWATCH 0.3 — MONITOR YOUR CCTV WALL ON YOUR PC

1. Right-click Framewatch-PC.zip and choose Extract All. Double-click the
   extracted Framewatch.html in current desktop Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
2. Leave your CCTV viewer open. Click Share screen and select the CCTV window
   or camera-wall tab, not Framewatch. No audio or camera passwords are needed.
3. Set columns x rows (up to 4 x 4). Crop away menus or sidebars. Camera 01
   is at the top left; numbers continue across each row.
4. Heavy is on by default. Every sweep runs TWO person models on EVERY usable
   camera and checks overlapping close-ups when there are enough pixels.
   Scanning a large wall can take seconds. See sweep progress and Last scan.
   On a slow PC, Balanced, Detailed and Fast remain available.
5. Open Vacancy dashboard. Name places lets you label cameras, for example
   Cabin A. Read Empty from, Last seen empty and Duration for each place.
   You can filter by place, date or layout, or Export CSV. Switching between
   Monitor and Vacancy dashboard in this tab keeps analysis running.
6. Stop sharing to end monitoring. Reopen this SAME extracted HTML in the SAME
   browser to see saved periods. Use Clear history to forget them. File storage
   varies by browser, profile and file path; export CSV before moving the app,
   clearing browser data, or ending a private browsing session.

WHAT THE TIMES MEAN
A period spans actual live checks with no person detected. A single check has
no measured duration. Times are local to this PC (CSV: UTC), NOT taken from the
CCTV overlay. The end is the last empty check, not the next person’s arrival.
Unclear/poor views, interrupted capture and long gaps break the period. Stop
and reshare if cameras change position in your CCTV viewer. Grid/crop changes
start a separate layout so names cannot transfer to the wrong place.

Use only screens you are allowed to monitor. Frames stay in memory. No video,
audio, face identity or images are recorded or uploaded. Only vacancy times,
status and layout labels are stored in your browser. No installer, server,
Node.js, internet connection or account is required.

QUICK CHECK / TROUBLESHOOTING
- Open image > Example camera grid.webp at 2 x 2 should show 01 and 03
  occupied, 02 and 04 empty. Stills and the sample NEVER create time periods.
- Keep Framewatch running and your PC awake. It cannot recover earlier times,
  see behind an obstruction, or tell if a DVR is replaying an old scene.
  Empty means no person detected, not proof a cabin was vacant.
- If browser/device policy blocks screen capture, contact your IT admin or
  use a screenshot for a one-time check. Do not disable security policies.
- If a detector cannot load, try current Edge or Chrome with WebGL available.
  A failed scan stays Unclear. Export important history before quitting.
